# Privacy Policy – Wealth tracker

_Last updated: 1 November 2026_

## Who we are
Wealth tracker is a private, non-commercial application for personal financial
overview. It is developed and used exclusively by its owner. It is not offered
to third parties.

Contact for data protection matters: JOUW-EMAIL@voorbeeld.nl

## What data is processed
With the explicit consent of the account holder, the following data is retrieved
through Enable Banking (enablebanking.com):
- account details (account name, IBAN, currency)
- account balances
- transaction history (date, amount, counterparty, description)

## Purpose
The data is used solely to give the account holder an overview of their own
income, expenses and wealth.

## Storage and sharing
- Data is stored locally on the owner's own device only.
- Data is not sold, shared with or disclosed to any third party.
- No tracking, analytics or advertising is used.

## Retention
Data is kept for as long as the owner uses the application and can be deleted
by the owner at any time.

## Your rights
Consent can be withdrawn at any time through the bank or Enable Banking. After
withdrawal, no new data will be retrieved. Under the GDPR you have the right to
access, correct and delete your data. Contact the address above for any request.
